The three tiers

Strongest evidence ↔ weakest evidence

01

Official promo

You can hear the track in a trailer or extended look posted by the publisher.

Prefer the official video. Polygon, Music Ally, GTA Wiki, and NME help name the song. Fan compilations and unnamed Discord lists are not enough on their own.

02

Rockstar-named

A Rockstar staff member names an artist in an interview, but not necessarily a song.

Stronger than a rumour. Weaker than hearing the record in a trailer.

03

Artist-reported

An artist or representative says they are involved, and we can tie that claim.

Link a primary post or interview they gave. Aggregators stay as discovery until that URL exists. This is not Rockstar-verified.

The hard no list

What we refuse

  • Rumours.
  • Aggregators as proof.
  • Anonymous screenshots.
  • Credits with no link.

Editorial rules

Link out, don't lift.

Cite official media and interviews. Link out. Do not host Rockstar media, lyrics, or copied bios.

Claim → find primary link → check context → label tier → publish with sources
